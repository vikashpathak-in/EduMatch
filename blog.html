How to Choose the Right Home Tutor for Your Child

Questions parents can ask before selecting a tutor, from subject fit and teaching style to communication and progress review.

By EduMatch Editorial Team · Updated 25 November 2026 · 5 min read

The right tutor should match a student's learning needs, subject level and schedule. Choosing only by distance or the lowest fee may overlook important factors such as teaching approach and communication.

1. Identify the learning requirement

Be clear about the class, subject and goal. A student may need help understanding concepts, regular homework support, exam preparation or confidence in a particular topic. Share the requirement before arranging a trial session.

2. Check subject and class-level experience

Ask which classes and subjects the tutor teaches and how they approach the relevant syllabus. A tutor's experience should match the student's current level and learning objective.

3. Discuss teaching style

Ask how the tutor explains difficult topics, checks understanding and handles questions. A good session should give the student opportunities to participate rather than only listen or copy notes.

4. Agree on practical details

  • Days, timings and expected session duration.
  • Home tuition or online learning arrangement.
  • Fees, payment frequency and cancellation expectations.
  • Study materials and homework responsibilities.
  • How parents will receive progress updates.

5. Arrange a trial or introductory discussion

Where possible, observe whether the student feels comfortable asking questions. A short introductory session can help both the family and tutor understand whether the arrangement is suitable.

6. Review progress regularly

Discuss what the student has covered, where they are still struggling and what to practise next. Look for improvement in understanding and confidence, not only marks from one test.

Questions parents can ask

  • Which subjects and classes do you currently teach?
  • How do you plan lessons for a student who is struggling?
  • How do you track progress and share feedback?
  • What happens if a class needs to be rescheduled?

EduMatch reminder: Compare tutors based on suitability, communication and the student's learning needs. Verify qualifications and references directly before finalising an arrangement.
